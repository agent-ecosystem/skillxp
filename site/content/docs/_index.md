skillxp stages an [Agent Skill](https://agentskills.io) on a real
harness, invokes it headlessly, and reports what actually reached the
model, with transcript evidence. These pages cover installation, the
use cases, the CLI, the Go library, sandboxing, and the per-harness
lore the profiles encode.
